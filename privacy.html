Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MeenAI ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit a contact or quote form, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, company, and information about your project. We use this only to respond to your enquiry and manage our work with you.

How we use your data

  • To reply to enquiries and prepare quotes
  • To deliver and support the services you engage us for
  • To send service-related communications. We do not sell your data, ever.

Analytics and cookies

If Google Analytics is enabled on this site, it collects anonymised usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type) to help us improve the site. You may block cookies in your browser without affecting core functionality.

Data storage and security

Enquiry data is stored on managed infrastructure with access restricted to the MeenAI team. We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to serve you or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to hello@meenai.in. We respond within 30 days.
