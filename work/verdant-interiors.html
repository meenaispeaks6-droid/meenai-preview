Verdant Interiors

An editorial portfolio site for a Delhi design studio, built to turn admiring visitors into booked consultations.

Website DesignDevelopmentCMS
VInterior Design26

The challenge

The studio relied on Instagram alone. Enquiries arrived unqualified, consultation calls were scattered across DMs and WhatsApp, and the team had no single place to present their portfolio seriously to commercial clients.

Our approach

We designed a typography-led portfolio around large project imagery and a six-step enquiry wizard that qualifies budget, scope and timeline before a call is booked. Leads route straight to WhatsApp with full context attached.

The outcome

Consultation requests now arrive pre-qualified, the studio presents a single link instead of a feed, and content updates happen in-house through the CMS.
