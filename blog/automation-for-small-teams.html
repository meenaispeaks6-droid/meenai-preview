Automation for small teams: start with the reply

You do not need an AI department. You need the two hours a day your team spends answering the same five questions.

When small businesses hear "AI automation" they picture robots replacing staff. The reality is smaller and more useful: the first automation worth building is the reply.

Every enquiry that waits two hours for an answer loses to the competitor who answered in two minutes. Nothing else in your funnel matters if the first touch is slow.

The three-automation starter set

  • Instant acknowledgement with a real answer to the top question, not "we'll get back to you"
  • Qualified routing: budget and timeline captured before a human looks at it
  • Follow-up that happens automatically when an enquiry goes quiet

That set typically recovers five to ten hours a week and closes the gap where most small businesses quietly lose revenue. Build that before you build anything clever.
